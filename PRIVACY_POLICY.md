# Privacy Policy for Khmer Food & Tourism

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026  
**Application Name:** Khmer Food & Tourism (អាហារ និងទេសចរណ៍ខ្មែរ)  
**Package ID:** `com.khmerfood.tourism`  
**Developer Contact:** support@khmerfoodtourism.com  

---

## 1. Overview
Khmer Food & Tourism ("we", "our", or "the App") is dedicated to promoting Cambodian culinary heritage and tourism destinations. We take your privacy seriously and adhere strictly to Google Play Store and Apple App Store privacy regulations.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide and improve our services:
- **Account Data:** Optional user profile data including display name, email address, user role, and avatar.
- **Restaurant Listings:** Business information voluntarily provided by restaurant owners, including restaurant name, address, telephone number, food menu items, photos, and operating hours.
- **Favorites & Bookmarks:** Dishes and destinations saved locally on your device for offline reading.
- **Transaction Records:** Reference IDs and verification timestamps for restaurant registration fees ($4.99 promotional tier). No credit card or banking PIN credentials are ever collected or stored on our servers.

## 3. Device Permissions & Purpose
- `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: To load high-resolution authentic culinary photography, tourist destinations, and verify restaurant registration status.
- `CAMERA`: Optional permission used only when a restaurant owner voluntarily takes a photo of their dish or payment receipt.
- `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (max API 32): Optional permission used only to select a photo from the device gallery for menu uploads.
- `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`: Optional permission used solely to calculate distance to nearby restaurants and destinations. We do not track your location in the background.

## 4. Security & Data Protection
- **Encryption in Transit:** All network requests strictly enforce HTTPS/TLS via Android Network Security Configuration. Plaintext HTTP traffic is blocked.
- **Password Security:** Administrator and account credentials are encrypted using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations and cryptographic salts.
- **Brute-Force Protection:** Account lockout mechanisms enforce 15-minute cool-downs after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.

## 5. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)
Users have the full right to delete their account and associated data:
- **In-App Deletion:** Navigate to **Profile > Settings > Privacy Policy & Data Safety > Delete My Account & Data**.
- **Web / Email Request:** Users can request permanent deletion of their account, uploaded restaurant listings, or reviews by emailing `support@khmerfoodtourism.com` with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All requested data will be deleted within 7 business days.

## 6. Third-Party Services
The App integrates trusted third-party services:
- **Unsplash CDN:** For delivery of authentic high-resolution food images.
- **Google Maps:** For optional map navigation to physical restaurant locations.
- **Bakong KHQR:** For voluntary promotional registration verification.

## 7. Children's Privacy
Our App contains culinary recipes and cultural tourism listings rated for all ages (Everyone / 3+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the App and at our official repository: https://github.com/igpubinfo-max/Khmer-Food-Tourism
