Privacy Policy — Cambodia Flavors & Tourism (រសជាតិ និងទេសចរណ៍កម្ពុជា)

Effective Date: October 22, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Cambodia Flavors & Tourism (រសជាតិ និងទេសចរណ៍កម្ពុជា) ("we", "our", or "the App", Android package ID com.khmerfood.tourism) is a culinary and travel guide application for Cambodia developed by Savuth Chea / Chea Kimseng. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application and web services.

1. Information We Collect (ព័ត៌មានដែលយើងប្រមូល)

  • Account & Profile Information: When you create an optional user account, we store your display name, username, email address or phone number, and profile preferences so you can save bookmarks, wishlists, and reviews.
  • Location Data (Approximate & Precise GPS): With your explicit permission, the App uses your device's location solely while the App is in use to calculate distances to nearby Cambodian restaurants, cafés, and tourist attractions ("Near Me" and Check-in features). We do not track your location in the background.
  • Camera & Photos: With your permission, the App accesses your camera or photo gallery only when you choose to scan a QR code, upload a review photo, or attach a restaurant or payment receipt photo.

2. How We Use Your Information (ការប្រើប្រាស់ព័ត៌មាន)

  • To display authentic Cambodian recipes, restaurants, cafés, and tourist destinations across all 25 provinces and municipalities of Cambodia.
  • To provide tailored dish suggestions and day-by-day travel itineraries.
  • To synchronize your saved bookmarks, trip plans, and restaurant listings across devices via encrypted cloud storage (Supabase HTTPS/TLS).

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services (ការចែករំលែកទិន្នន័យ)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. The App communicates over encrypted HTTPS connections with:

  • Google Maps Platform & OpenStreetMap: To display interactive maps, coordinates, and place details in Cambodia.
  • Supabase Cloud Database: To securely synchronize user accounts, restaurant listings, and saved preferences.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion (ការលុបគណនី និងទិន្នន័យ)

You have full control over your data. You can delete your saved bookmarks, wishlists, trip plans, or user account at any time directly inside the App (Profile → Account Settings) or by emailing us at igpub.info@gmail.com with the subject Delete My Account. Upon request, all associated personal data is permanently deleted within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy (ឯកជនភាពកុមារ)

Our App is a general-audience food and tourism guide and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
